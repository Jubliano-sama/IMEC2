## Packet Envelope and Framing

A participant experiences one physical click, but the system carries several different binary objects afterward. Compact UWB wake, discovery, and DS-TWR frames use their own layouts; reports, commands, survey records, routing data, and acknowledgements use the shared IMEC packet envelope, which is then wrapped by the UWB mesh or a host-facing stream ([UWB+BLE Protocols and Strategies 0.3.12.2.md:256-258](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/Documentation/UWB%2BBLE%20Protocols%20and%20Strategies%200.3.12.2.md#L256-L258)). The shared-packet codec consequently accepts report, mesh, command, survey, and error message classes, while compact UWB message IDs are handled by the UWB codecs instead ([protocol.c:76-114](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L76-L114)).

### Shared binary envelope

Every multi-byte value is serialized little-endian. The length byte at offset 27 selects one of two canonical header forms: payloads below `0xFF` use a 32-byte header, while payloads of `0xFF` bytes or more place the 16-bit length at offsets 28-29 and move packet age to offsets 30-33, producing a 34-byte header ([protocol.c:27-74](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L27-L74), [protocol.c:140-160](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L140-L160)).

| 3 | 1 | Flags | Carries control, routing, acknowledgement, diagnostic, and click semantics ([protocol.h:104-113](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L104-L113)). |

| 20-23 | 4 | Session ID | Correlates the packet with its click, command, collection, or survey session ([protocol.h:384-394](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L384-L394)). |

| Packet tail | 2 | CRC-16 | CCITT-FALSE, initialized to `0xFFFF` with polynomial `0x1021`, over the header and payload ([protocol.c:5-24](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L5-L24), [protocol.c:158-164](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L158-L164)). |

Decoding is fail-closed: it rejects a short buffer, wrong magic or version, a noncanonical extended header for a payload below 255 bytes, a payload above 958 bytes, any total-length mismatch, a bad CRC, or an unrecognized shared message type before returning a packet ([protocol.c:169-235](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L169-L235)).

### COBS host framing

`serial_frame_encode_packet()` first creates a complete standard shared packet, COBS-encodes it, and appends a zero delimiter. Its decoder requires that final delimiter, reverses COBS, validates the complete shared packet, and only then copies the payload to the caller ([serial_frame.c:5-40](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/serial_frame.c#L5-L40), [serial_frame.c:42-83](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/serial_frame.c#L42-L83)). The helper allocates `PACKET_MAX_LEN`, so it covers the common packet allocation through a 255-byte payload; full 958-byte records use extended-capacity owners instead of silently overflowing this framing buffer ([serial_frame.c:11-29](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/serial_frame.c#L11-L29), [protocol.h:18-21](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L18-L21)).

## Messages, Flags, and TLVs

The message namespace follows the participant-to-research journey: compact UWB contact and ranging establish a measurement, reports carry it toward the gateway, mesh messages preserve delivery, commands operate the deployed system, and survey messages establish anchor geometry. The enum reserves retired values instead of reusing them, which keeps older captures distinguishable and prevents stale formats from being decoded as current traffic ([protocol.h:45-102](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L45-L102)).

| Family | Representative wire IDs | What they carry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact and compact UWB control | `WAKE_CLAIM` `0x08`, `DISCOVER` `0x09`, `RANGE_SCHEDULE` `0x0B`, `UWB_MESH` `0x0C` | Wake ownership, anchor discovery, ranging authorization, and the outer mesh carrier ([protocol.h:45-54](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L45-L54)). |
| DS-TWR and radio diagnostics | `POLL` `0x10` through pair result `0x19` | Identity-bound ranging exchanges and compact radio results ([protocol.h:55-64](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L55-L64)). |
| Participant and device reports | `CLICK_REPORT` `0x20`, `SELF_TEST_REPORT` `0x21`, `ANCHOR_HEARTBEAT` `0x22` | Research measurements, explicit self-test outcomes, and anchor health ([protocol.h:66-68](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L66-L68)). |
| Routing and reliable delivery | `MESH_DATA` `0x30` through `GATEWAY_ROUTE_REQ` `0x3F` | Payload forwarding, hop progress, gateway completion, routes, negotiated events, and busy responses; `0x33` and `0x34` remain reserved ([protocol.h:70-84](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L70-L84)). |
| Commands and collected results | `COMMAND` `0x40` through collection EACK `0x45` | Target operations, correlated results, offers/grants, bundles, and collection acknowledgement ([protocol.h:86-91](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L86-L91)). |
| Anchor survey and host progress | `SURVEY_REACH_REQ` `0x50` through `GATEWAY_COMMAND_EVENT` `0x56` | Reachability, pair control and results, discovery reports, and command-lifecycle telemetry ([protocol.h:93-100](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L93-L100)). |

The flag byte is a set of independent protocol assertions: `CONTROL_FOLLOWUP`, `ROUTE_SETUP`, `GATEWAY_ACK_REQUIRED`, `GATEWAY_ACK`, `DIAGNOSTIC`, `COUNT_AS_CLICK`, `ERROR`, and `RANGE_ONLY` occupy all eight bits ([protocol.h:104-113](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L104-L113)). A normal range report must select exactly one of click or diagnostic mode, always requests a gateway ACK, uses TTL 4, and starts with packet age zero; the constructor rejects missing identities, zero session/sequence, or both modes together ([report.c:482-512](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/report.c#L482-L512)).

### TLV payload model

Each payload field is `type:u8`, `length:u8`, then `length` bytes. Numeric appenders encode 16-, 32-, and 64-bit values little-endian, and a single TLV value is therefore bounded to 255 bytes ([protocol.h:22-26](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L22-L26), [protocol.c:237-297](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L237-L297)). `tlv_find()` skips fields it does not seek, but rejects a missing TLV header or any declared value that overruns the payload; message-specific readers then require exact widths for mandatory integer fields ([protocol.c:299-413](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L299-L413)). This is the compatibility rule: additive unknown or optional TLVs can pass through, while malformed lengths and missing mandatory identity fields cannot.

## Identity, Correlation, and Time

The protocol does not rely on one global timestamp to decide whether records belong together. It carries layered identities so that the [end-to-end click path](02_one-click-end-to-end.md), the [mesh delivery path](05_connected-routing-and-reliable-delivery.md), and the [survey workflow](07_anchor-self-setup-survey-and-geometry.md) can each reject cross-run traffic at their own boundary.

| Layer | Identity tuple or field | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared packet | `src_id`, `dst_id`, `session_id`, `seq` | Relays can age and forward a packet without changing the original application identity; the fields are fixed members of every shared packet ([protocol.h:384-394](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L384-L394)). |
| Participant range report | `clicker_id`, `anchor_id`, `event_seq`, sender-local `timestamp_ms`, plus optional attempt/detection evidence | The builder requires distinct nonzero device IDs and quality at most 100 before appending measurement fields ([report.h:71-93](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/report.h#L71-L93), [report.c:246-299](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/report.c#L246-L299)). |
| Fragmented distance aggregate | Total `sample_count`, chunk `sample_index`, distance values, round indices, and aligned sequence-start timestamps | A chunk must fit inside the declared total and contains at most 13 aligned samples; malformed bounds or missing parallel arrays are rejected ([report.h:13-17](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/report.h#L13-L17), [report.c:15-89](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/report.c#L15-L89)). |
| Command result | `gateway_id`, `gateway_epoch`, `command_seq`, `node_id`, `node_boot_counter`, `result_seq` | This composite separates gateway generations, commands, node reboots, and repeated results instead of treating one sequence number as globally unique ([protocol.h:396-403](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L396-L403), [protocol.c:415-478](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L415-L478)). |

## Capacity and Compatibility Contracts

Packet capacity is a cross-role contract rather than a suggestion. The generic codec supports an extended 958-byte payload, but individual queues and transports deliberately accept smaller sets; a caller must use the correct owner instead of assuming that codec capacity implies end-to-end admission.

| Boundary | Enforced capacity | Consequence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Compact-length shared header | 0-254 payload bytes, 32-byte header | Payload `0xFF` is the extended-length sentinel, so 255 bytes switches to the 34-byte form ([protocol.h:14-21](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L14-L21), [protocol.c:66-74](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L66-L74)). |
| Common packet allocation | Up to 255 payload bytes; 291 bytes including extended header and CRC at that boundary | The COBS serial helper uses this allocation, so a larger packet fails capacity checks rather than truncating ([protocol.h:18-21](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L18-L21), [serial_frame.c:11-31](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/serial_frame.c#L11-L31)). |
| Extended shared packet | 958 payload bytes; 994 bytes with header and CRC | The core encoder and decoder reject anything larger ([protocol.h:20-21](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/include/protocol.h#L20-L21), [protocol.c:122-138](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L122-L138)). |

Compatibility is explicit at every boundary. The fixed envelope version must match exactly, retired message IDs remain reserved, extended lengths below 255 are rejected as noncanonical, and required TLVs must have their exact widths ([protocol.c:169-220](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L169-L220), [protocol.c:335-413](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/protocol.c#L335-L413)). Additive optional TLVs are the supported evolution path: for example, attempt index and detection source may be absent in older valid click records, while new firmware emits both together when that evidence is present ([UWB+BLE Protocols and Strategies 0.3.12.2.md:43-51](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/Documentation/UWB%2BBLE%20Protocols%20and%20Strategies%200.3.12.2.md#L43-L51), [report.c:276-291](https://github.com/Jubliano-sama/IMEC2/blob/f6594e41b57f5fd612aba182e0bd13cbbdd0c621/firmware/src/report.c#L276-L291)).
